# Visual Governance Review Protocol (Draft)

For each proposed interface or visualization, record:

1. **Decision represented:** what is being decided or displayed?
2. **Authority:** who or what is permitted to act?
3. **Evidence:** which sources support the state shown?
4. **Consent and scope:** which people or data are included, and under what authority?
5. **Reversibility:** can the action be paused, reviewed, or rolled back?
6. **Audit trail:** what event, timestamp, version, and outcome are retained?
7. **Accessibility:** can the state be read without color alone?

Do not visually present a proposal as law, an allegation as a finding, or a simulated result as empirical evidence.
